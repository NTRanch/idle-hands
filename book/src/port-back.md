# Port-Back — Idle Hands

Filed 2026-10-30 at the v1.0 build. The full filing lives in
`docs/PORT-BACK.md`; this chapter carries the verdict and the numbers.

- **Verdict:** plays. The idle IS the performance: breath, blinks,
  gaze springs and fidgets run between inputs, and neglect visibly
  changes the actor — posture sink, slower blinks — including at the
  start of the next visit. The Descent-trace-as-behaviour idea works:
  her fidgets characterise her without a line of text.
- **Numbers:** breath loop 3.8 s; blink 2.4–5.2 s ×(1 + loneliness),
  close 130 ms; gaze chase stiffness ~6/s, hair/sleeve springs
  2.6–3.2/s; fidget quiet threshold 6 s; look-settle 900 ms;
  performance 0.027 ms/step, wasm 40,489 bytes.
- **Language verdict (Odin):** data-oriented layout suits actor sims;
  the whole core audits in one read and the wasm output is clean.
  Costs: the test-file collision (breakage B-2), no nested procs,
  exhaustive switches. Odin stays in the rotation for sim cores.
